# mission_1

## diagnosis
For this I will be comparing normal_1 and dim_1. Both of these have the box on the mug, the main difference is that the center of the box and where it would be essentially "identifying" the mug is different. In the dim_1 the center is a lot further away from the mug than in normal_1. When looking at dim_1, the mask lost some of the cup itself and the center of its shadow on the table, it was harder for me to discern in the contour image which one the cup was because of the split basically in the middle. However, I narrowed it down to 18, which has a high area in the table, being 7,532px^2. Dimming the light made the shadow from the mug a lot darker and that pushed the mug's pixels outside my thresholds. The lower lighting lowers the mug's brightness and my lower bound for saturation is 130, so it may reject its pixels. Normal_1's raw mask the mug is mostly white, but the shadow is also white, making it appear as one whole object. The area for this region is 13,55px^2, which is pretty big as well, but the box and center was closer to the mug but still on the desk. Since the color of the mug and shadow are very similar my saturation range couldn't separate them. 

## limitations
One of the parameters I changed was the lower saturation bound, I changed it from 70 to 130. In the raw mask, most of the white region that was to the left side of the mug had become black, while the mug was white. This happened because by having this new bound I rejected all the pixel saturation that was between 70 and 130, which are the grayer and more washed-out ones. I was adjusting this value and when I raised it too high it removed majority of the cup so this range was safer. Now if this cup was a bright red the cup would pass easily because it would be high in saturation and the shadow wouldn't be similar in terms of saturation.
