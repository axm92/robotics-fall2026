## Response

This activity made me think about how much truly goes into robotics. I already knew there were a lot of components but the way it can be broken down and tested even without a physical robot is pretty cool. I was originally interested in this class because of the robotics team I was on in high school. During that time, I was more involved in the engineering process rather than the computing part and I now would like to know more about the code behind the robot. I have always been interested in computing that has a physical outcome rather than one seen on a screen. What I didn't take into account is how that comes with a lot of procedures to ensure safety because if something doesn't work as intended it could be harmful to those around it. While this is still interesting to me I did find timeouts/constraints involving time or proper connections between certain systems to be quite annoying. If one thing wasn't properly connected the whole thing wouldn't work. Which is good because then you can find what to fix but at times it became a lot. I think once I become more familiar with the commands and terminology it will come a lot more natural but for this activity I had slight trouble.
