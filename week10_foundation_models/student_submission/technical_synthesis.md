I learned that it takes very specific requests or commands to have a response from a robot that is useful and accurate. If you are too vague the robot will most likely behave in a way that is not useful or not even the intended behavior. However, if you give too much information the robot may not be able to process everything and will still not behave in a way that is useful. It is important to find a balance between specific but not too specific to the point where the robot just does whatever to fill in information it doesn't have. For example, my requests in mission 1 and mission 2 made meaningful impacts and gave better results than the ones modeled but in the last mission I couldn't really find that balance. The robot needs a more guided route than the one I provided. 

In mission 1, I used specific vocabulary that was within the card given at the beginning and that resulted in a more concise and helpful response. In mission 2, I gave an explanation of what the goal of the robot was so that it had more information on what kind of tool was actually needed, and it provided me with the correct tool, even though it doesn't confirm that that is the tool in the image. In mission 3, I was as specific as I could get but tried to keep it short as well, however it still got stuck and didn't make it to the end goal.
